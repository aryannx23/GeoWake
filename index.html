ABOUT THE WEB APP

Location-Based Travel Alarm: Wake Up When You Arrive

"Wake up when you reach your destination, not at a fixed time."

GeoWake is built for travelers, commuters, and students who want to sleep peacefully during transit without worrying about missing their stop. Traditional alarms wake you at a predetermined time, but trains and buses frequently experience delays or arrive unexpectedly early. GeoWake replaces clock-based guesswork with smart background GPS geofencing—calculating your real-time distance and triggering a loud, persistent alarm the moment you cross your chosen arrival radius.

Customizable Alert Perimeter

Search any railway station, bus terminal, or drop a pin on the map. Choose your preferred wake-up boundary from 100 meters up to 5 kilometers.

Battery-Adaptive Tracking

Conserves battery on long trips with intelligent adaptive polling: low frequency while far away, seamlessly transitioning to high-precision GPS as you approach.

Anti-False Alarm Filtering

Built-in accuracy hysteresis and jump rejection prevent rogue GPS glitches or satellite drift from triggering accidental wake-ups mid-journey.

Screen-Locked Persistent Audio

Engineered to wake deep sleepers through loud synthesized alarms and continuous vibration that persist until you physically wake up and dismiss the alert.

Real-World Example: Traveling by train on an overnight journey? Set your destination to your arrival station with a 500m perimeter. Lock your phone, sleep peacefully, and GeoWake sounds the alert before your stop!

How GeoWake Works
